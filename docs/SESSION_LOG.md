## Level 11 Direction

Level 11 was designed to avoid fake repetition of the existing boss ladder.

The accepted identity became:

**Head Hunter Boss**

Target design meaning:

- the boss should create a meaningful later scoring opportunity
- the player should be pushed toward head-focused conversion
- the encounter should not collapse into a specific-item check
- the encounter should not collapse into a body-route judgment repeat
- the encounter should not collapse into an anti-predictability repeat
- the encounter should not merely become “more throws required”

This established a new boss demand without adding a new goal type or a new architecture branch.

---

## Authoring Rule

Level 11 followed the existing authoritative content path:

**pattern → state window profile → preset → roster entry → level selection → runtime slot routing**

The content was not solved through:

- scene-only defense edits
- fake manual slot tweaking without roster closure
- new goal-type churn
- parallel runtime injection logic

This was an important confirmation that the current architecture was already sufficient.

---

## Initial Problem

The first authored pass did not produce a convincing boss.

Observed issue:

- the encounter was too permissive
- defensive presence was too low
- the player could often win quickly with little real pressure
- the boss identity did not feel materially different enough

Important lesson:

A theoretically correct concept is not enough.
If the defensive pressure is too weak, the identity does not exist in practice.

---

## Correction

The fix was not to add a new system.

The fix direction was:

- increase defensive presence
- make the boss more willing to establish pressure
- keep the identity data-authored
- preserve the current architecture
- avoid converting the encounter into a timed auto-cycle gimmick

The goal was not to create a robotic loop.
The goal was to make the later scoring opportunity matter.

---

## Validation Result

After the pressure increase, the encounter was accepted as basically correct.

Acceptance meaning:

- the boss felt materially more real
- the level no longer collapsed into generic safe spam
- the player could feel the need to respect the scoring opportunity
- the Level 11 identity became distinct enough to keep

The important outcome was not final release balance.
The important outcome was identity closure.

## Level 11 Closure Rule

Level 11 is now treated as closed content at the identity level.

Do not reopen it casually.

Only revisit if runtime testing later proves one of the following:

- the head-focused later-window identity is no longer readable
- the encounter collapses into another boss identity after future balance changes
- repository content drifts away from the validated authoring chain
- later presentation work reveals a real clarity problem

Otherwise, preserve the current implementation as part of the validated boss-reference ladder.
